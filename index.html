Bosque River Review

You have reached the online library for this publication.

The Bosque River Review is a new literary journal associated with Tarleton State University’s Department of English & Languages. The journal is named for the 104-mile river originating in Stephenville, Texas. For each issue, undergraduate student editors curate and publish submitted art in prose and poetry.
